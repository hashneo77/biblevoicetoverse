TAYA - Canticle
==================

A
Christ, as a light
Illumine and guide me
Christ, as a shield
Overshadow me
Christ under me
Christ over me
Christ beside me
On my left and my right

A
Christ as a light
Christ as a shield
Christ when I’m standing
Christ when I kneel
Christ here beside me
On my left and my right
Christ all around me
All my days and my nights
