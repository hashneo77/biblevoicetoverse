ENGLISH VERSION (Thuye Tune – Karthave Mama Rajave)

All Those Upright And Righteous

Psalms 63, 64, 65 (Marmisa 2,3 24)

R
As The Parched Land Thirsts And Cries

For The Waters To Be Quenched

I, Too, Dwell And Wait For You

O Lord, For You Are My God.

🎵🎵🎵

A
On This Earth The Righteous Ones

Will Thrive, Blossom, And Bear Fruit

Like The Cedars Of Lebanon

Rooted In His Holy Courts.

🎵🎵🎵

R
The Righteous Ones Will Rejoice

Always In The Lord, Their God

They Will Place Their Trust In You

Those Who Are Pure Will Sing Praise.

🎵🎵🎵

A
O Lord Blessed Are The Ones

Worthy To Find Your Favour

Your Hand, Surely, Will Lead Them

To Live In Your Dwelling Place.

🎵🎵🎵

R
He Will Rejoice All The Days

In The Grace And Holiness

Of Your Awesome House, O Lord

And Your Fearsome Righteousness.

🎵🎵🎵

A
Glory To The Father, Son

And Ruha D’Qudsha

From All Ages To Ages

Now And Forever, Amen.
