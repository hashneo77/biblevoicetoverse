Puqdankon &#038; Thesbohtha (Suriyani)
======================================

Puqdankon is the Aramaic (East Syriac) version of the Song Anna Pesaha Thirunnallil and Thesbohtha Lalaha is the Aramaic (East Syriac) version of the Song Athyunnathamaam Swarlokathil

Note : The following hymn lyrics are written to make singing vocally easier. Characters have been re-organized / changed to sing with the near correct pronunciation.
