Rajavaya Karthave (Uyirppukalam)
================================

A
കുരിശില്‍ മൃതനായി

കല്ലറ പൂകിയവന്‍

ഉത്ഥാനം ചെയ്‌തു.

ഉന്നതനായ് സര്‍വാധിപനായ്

മഹിമാപൂരിത രാജാവായ്

അഖിലരെയും വിധിചെയ്‌തീടാനായ്.

ENGLISH VERSION (Mishiha Karthavin Thirumey Ninavumitha)

O Lord, my King, I will extol You (Season of Resurrection)

S
O Lord, my King, I will extol You.

A
One who had died on

The cross was entombed

And He is risen.

The Lord of all and most high

M’shiha, will reign in Heaven

As the King in great splendour

He in time will come again

To judge all people in the world.
