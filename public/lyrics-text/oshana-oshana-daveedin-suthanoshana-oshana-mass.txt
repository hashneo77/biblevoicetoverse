Oshana Oshana Daveedin Suthanoshana (Oshana Mass)
=================================================

There are 3 varieties of this Song.Song 1 : Oshana – Karthavin Poojitha Naamathil

Song 2 : Oshana – Sehiyon Puthri Modham Punaruka

Song 3 : Oshana – Karthavin Thiru Naamathil

The below lyrics is from the Updated Taksa (2022).
Song 2 has a small change – Ponnin became Vinnin in the new Taksa.
