ENGLISH VERSION

We call upon you, Lord of all,

Pour out in plenty upon this people,

Mercy and blessings

Never decreasing, unceasing

from your treasury,

We know, O Lord of all,

That you are the one who saved us from sin,

That you are kind-hearted and merciful
