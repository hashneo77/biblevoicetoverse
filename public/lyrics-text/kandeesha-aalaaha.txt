Kandeesha Aalaaha (Suriyani)
============================

Kandeesha Aalaaha is the Aramaic (East Syriac) version of the Song Shabdhamuyarthi Paadiduvin (Trisagion)

(Scroll down / click to view the English Version)

Note : The following hymn lyrics are written to make singing vocally easier. Characters have been re-organized / changed to sing with the near correct pronunciation.
