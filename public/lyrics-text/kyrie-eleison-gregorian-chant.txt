Kyrie Eleison (Gregorian Chant)
===============================

“Kyrie Elieson” is a simple prayer that is used in the liturgies of traditional Western and Eastern Christianity. This phrase is the Greek translation of “Lord have mercy” in English.

Note : The following lyrics are written to make singing vocally easier. Characters have been re-organized / changed to sing with the near correct pronunciation.
