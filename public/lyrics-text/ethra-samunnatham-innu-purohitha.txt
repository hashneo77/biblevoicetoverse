ENGLISH VERSION

How eminent O priest is this role

Today you have undertaken

Heavenly ministers of fire and spirit

Are struck with wonder and awe

Gabriel, is ever acclaimed through the ages

And Holy Mi-cha-el, most exalted

Are worthy of their names

They testify to this truth

Yet, when compared

To the lineage of priests this day,

The angelic hosts of God in all their splendor

Seem so meager in semblance!
