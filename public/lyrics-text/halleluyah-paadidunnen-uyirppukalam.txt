Halleluyah Paadidunnen (Uyirppukalam)

Let Us All Sing Halleluia (Season of Resurrection)

A
Let us all sing halleluia

Halleluia, halleluia.

A
Let us all sing halleluia

Halleluia, halleluia.

M
The Lord’s reign is eternal

Let the entire earth rejoice.

F
The light clouds and thick darkness

They gather around the Lord.

A
He founded His throne in justice

And in holiness forever.

S
Glory to the Father and Son

and Ruha d’Qudsha.

From all ages to all ages

Now and forever,

A
Amen

Let us all sing halleluia

Halleluia, halleluia

A
Let us all sing halleluia
