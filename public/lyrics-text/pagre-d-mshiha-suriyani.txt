Pagre D&#8217; Mshiha (Suriyani)
================================

Pagre D’ Mshiha is the Aramaic (East Syriac) version of the Song Karthavil Njan Drudamayi Sharanapettu.

Note : The following hymn lyrics are written to make singing vocally easier. Characters have been re-organized / changed to sing with the near correct pronunciation.

I have firmly trusted in the Lord.

The precious Body and Blood of Christ is on the holy altar.

Let us all approach Him with reverence and awe,
