Nadha Sakalesha (Uyirppukalam)
==============================

ഉയിര്‍പ്പുകാലം

ഏകീകൃതരൂപത്തിലുള്ള വിശുദ്ധ കുര്‍ബാന (Nov 28, 2021)

കൈവിട്ടില്ലെന്നെ.

ENGLISH VERSION (Ambaramanavaratham Tune)

The Lord, God of All (Season of Resurrection)

R
The Lord, God of all

Did not abandon

Me among the dead.

R
With hymns of the Ruha

Hallel, halleluia

Remember on this altar

Resurrection of the Lord

Come let us, now, celebrate.
