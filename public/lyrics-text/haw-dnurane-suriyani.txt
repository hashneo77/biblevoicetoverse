M
He Who Quakes The Blazing Angels

With His Countenance:

He Is Seen As Bread And Wine Up

On The Altar.

F
Lightning Vested Angels Burn In

Glancing At Him;

Wretched Dust Is Bold Unveiled As

It Consumes Him.

M
Myst’ries Of The Son Of God Are

Fire In Heav’n Above,

And So Isaiah, Testifies Who

Once Beheld Them.

F
Mysteries Which Are And Had Been

In The Godhead,

For Adam’s Sons Are Now Divided

On The Paten.

M
The Earthly Altar, Here Is Like The

Throne Of Angels,

And All The Ranks And Hosts Of Heaven

Come Surround It.

F
The Body Of The Son Of God Is

On The Altar,

And Adam’s Sons Come In Procesion

Now To Hold It.

M
And Here The Priest Is Standing Like A

Man In Linen,

While Giving Pearls And Gems Of Beauty

To The Needy.

F
If Ever There Was Envy Known A-

Mong The Angels,

The Cherubim May Envy Men Who

Take Communion.

M
Where Once The Wood Of Crucifixion

Stood In Zion,

There Now Has Grown The, Tree Producing

Fruit Eternal.

F
Where Once The Nails Were, Fastened In The

Hands Of Jesus,

There Too The Binding, Ropes Of Isaac

Who Was Offered.

M
O Welcome Now, O Priest Instructed

By The Spirit,

Who Carries In His, Hand The Keys To

God’s Own Dwelling!

F
O Welcome Now, O Priest Absolving

Man Below Here,

While God Above Himself Absolves Him!

Halleluiah!

A
Halleluiah, Halleluiah

Halleluiah!

Halleluiah, Halleluiah

Halleluiah!
