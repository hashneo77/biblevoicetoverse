ENGLISH VERSION (Halleluia Padidunnen)

Let Us All Sing Halleluia (Season of Lent)

A
Let us all sing halleluia

Halleluia, halleluia.

A
Let us all sing halleluia

Halleluia, halleluia.

M
Lord I purified my hands

In the waters of Your grace.

F
And in praise and adoration

Walked around Your holy altar.

A
I love the service of Your house

And the throne of Your glory.

S
Glory to the Father and Son

and Ruha d’Qudsha.

From all ages to all ages

Now and forever,

A
Amen

Let us all sing halleluia

Halleluia, halleluia

A
Let us all sing halleluia

Halleluia, halleluia

The English version of the song is used "ad experimentum" from the Syro-Malabar Synodal Commission for Liturgy, as part of the Updated Holy Qurbana Taksa.
